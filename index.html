Find Your New Best Friend

Welcome to Happy Paws! We help friendly dogs and cats find safe, caring and permanent homes. Adopting a pet gives an animal a second chance and brings a loyal companion into your life.

Take your time, learn about each animal, and choose a companion whose needs fit your home and lifestyle.

Why adopt a pet?

  • You give an animal a loving second chance.
  • You welcome a loyal friend into your family.
  • You support responsible animal care.
  • You help make space for another animal in need.
